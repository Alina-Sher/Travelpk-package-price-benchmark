# Travel.pk | Competitor Package Price Benchmark
*Research date: 21 October 2026. Prepared for TechAbout management review.*

## 1. Executive summary

Travel.pk's package index lists **12 packages**, and every one is shown as **"Tailored quote"** with no public price. Only 1 detail page (Hunza) was individually inspected; it confirms the page is a sample itinerary, "not a live or pre-priced offer". **0 Travel.pk prices are publicly available**, so a genuine "cheaper or costlier" verdict **cannot be determined** for any package from public information.

The research recorded **23 competitor offers** across Apricot Tours, Sastaticket, TourRadar listing (operator not captured). Of these, **10** carry a competitor price read directly from an opened page (4 of them contain an inconsistency on the page itself); 1 is an unconfirmed-basis price and 12 were seen only in search-result excerpts and are not counted as verified.

## 4. Public pricing availability findings

- **Travel.pk:** all 12 packages show "Tailored quote"; the Hunza detail page states there is no fixed price and that it is a sample planning route rather than a live offer. Price comparison therefore depends on internal quotes.
- **Sastaticket:** publishes "starting from" prices per person on double-sharing for many packages, mostly without stating flight inclusion on the card.
- **Apricot Tours:** publishes PKR and USD prices; the basis of the PKR figure for Hunza is not stated (see section 5).
- **Bookme:** no comparable package pricing was found in the research; it is recorded as not verified, not as absent.
- **No competitor offer found** for: Naran & Kaghan Valley Tour Package.

**Apricot Tours Hunza price (rechecked).** The detail page lists PKR 85,000 and US$1,290 (US$1,390 in other seasons). PKR 85,000 is identical across four seasonal rows and is not a conversion of the USD figure. The page separates inclusions for foreign and for Pakistani travellers but does not say whether PKR 85,000 is per person or per group, nor the room occupancy, hotel category or dates it applies to. It is therefore recorded as *Unconfirmed basis* and is not used as a confirmed price.

**Key scope differences.** Dubai (Sastaticket) excludes flights and visa while Travel.pk lists a flight component; Maldives and several international listings do not state flight inclusion; durations of Thailand, Malaysia and Azerbaijan teasers are shorter than Travel.pk's samples; TourRadar listings are in USD and cover 13–14 day itineraries.

## 6. Research methodology and limitations

- Travel.pk's package index and the Hunza detail page were opened and read. The other 11 detail pages were **not** individually inspected.
- Competitor pages were located by web search and opened where noted in the "Source type" column of the dataset. Items marked *Search excerpt (page not opened)* were taken from search-result excerpts only.
- No price was estimated, interpolated or converted. USD prices are shown in USD; no exchange rate was applied.
- A verdict is produced only when a valid Travel.pk quote exists, the competitor price is verified, currency and price basis match, and comparability is Medium or High. Differences within ±1% are reported as "Same price". Absolute difference = Travel.pk − competitor; percentage = difference ÷ competitor × 100.
- Competitor prices are dynamic, "starting from" or subject to availability and may have changed after the research date.
- Bookme could not be verified; TourRadar listings were seen only as excerpts and the operator was not captured.
- A search result for a site presenting itself as 'Best Tour Operator 2024' on a lovable.app domain, and Indian-market (Pickyourtrail) itineraries, were excluded as unverifiable or outside the Pakistani market.

## 7. Recommendations for obtaining comparable prices

1. **Obtain internal Travel.pk quotes** for each package under one specification: dates, number of travellers, double-sharing rooms, hotel star category, flight inclusion, meals. Enter them in `data/travelpk_quotes.csv`; the dashboard and report then compute verdicts automatically.
2. **Match competitor specifications to those quotes**: request or open itemised competitor quotes for the same dates and hotel tier, starting with Sastaticket (Skardu, Swat, Dubai, Turkey, Maldives) where verified prices exist.
3. **Open the competitor detail pages** not yet inspected (Sastaticket Thailand, Malaysia and Azerbaijan; Apricot Tours Skardu/Hunza/Gilgit-Baltistan tables).
4. **Confirm the Apricot Tours Hunza basis** (per person or per group, occupancy, hotel tier, dates) directly with the operator before using PKR 85,000.
5. **Add competitors with public package pricing** for Naran & Kaghan and Fairy Meadows, where no comparable offer was found.
6. **Consider publishing 'from' prices** with stated basis on Travel.pk package pages, which would make future benchmarking possible from public data.
7. **Recheck seasonally** and within days of any pricing decision, since prices are dynamic.
